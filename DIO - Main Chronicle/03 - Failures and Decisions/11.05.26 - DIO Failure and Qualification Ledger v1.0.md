## Non-erasure rule

1. Record every observed failure, refusal, unexpected behavior, unknown and review BLOCK with its original source, timing when known, actor/reviewer attribution and confidence.
2. Keep **what failed** separate from **what a later patch appears to repair**. A later source PASS does **not erase** a prior failure or establish a runtime PASS.
3. Record negative results and no-ops with the same care as positive results. Unknown, not run, blocked and inconclusive are **not** synonymous with PASS.
4. Preserve immutable source IDs (commit, Git blob, issue/comment ID); identify Git SHA-1 separately from independently recomputed raw-byte SHA-256.
5. Disclose what was reported by DIO via the user versus what an external reviewer authenticated directly. No impersonated or invented independent signature.
6. Distinguish intended future PS5 experimentation from the synthetic tests; containment work does **not** imply a jailbreak, PS5 connection or real-device approval.
7. No retroactive edits to historic verdicts. Add dated corrective evidence and cross-links, not revisions that hide a prior BLOCK.

## Root-cause interpretation (not erased findings)

The biggest strategic failure was not that the reviewer reported security blockers. The failure was **handoff completeness**: early documents described only the guardian, not what the guardian protected *for*. That makes it possible for an examiner to optimize the wrong goal without making an error within the assigned narrow evidence.

The correction is a shared **parent North Star** with two separate workstreams:
- **PS5 feasibility research only:** external supervision, conditional native/homebrew/Linux and jailbreak feasibility, each with firmware-specific evidence, safety, legal/account constraints and independent human gate.
- **CERBERUS containment research:** synthetic unit contracts, network/file/credential controls, independent A0/D/C custody, recovery, STOP and evidence; separately reviewed before device interfacing.

**Neither track authorizes device modification.** Research may find that the preferred approach does not require jailbreaking, or that no supported native route exists for the user firmware. A safe negative finding is progress.

## 4. Correction to earlier narrative

The third-party summary was **directionally correct** about official firmware availability, Sony OSS, community indices and the existence of tools and older captures. It requires four explicit constraints:

1. **Build versus public release:** `2026_1027` is the listed 14.22 build date; multiple November 2 publications report the rollout. Sony's source confirms the version/notes but does not itself give that exact rollout date.
2. **Open source versus proprietary:** the Sony OSS listing provides selected components, **not** full kernel/hypervisor/source. An OSS WebKit package labeled 14.00 onward is not evidence of a PS5 14.22 exploit or decrypted firmware.
3. **Encrypted versus decrypted:** `ps5-pup-unpacker` requires **previously decrypted** inputs. The companion decryptor is privileged-console-dependent with version constraints. Public availability of `PS5UPDATE.PUP` cannot establish a 14.22 executable-code extraction path.
4. **Older artifact versus current target:** the 13.60 kernel reconstruction is partial and community-asserted. It is a potential reference for older code/layout only, not a verifiable 14.22 kernel. Its declared SHA-256 and 688 missing pages remain source assertions, not our independently observed outputs.

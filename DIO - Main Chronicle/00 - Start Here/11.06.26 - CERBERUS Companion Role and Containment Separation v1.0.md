## Authoritative role correction — ORIENT-003

**CERBERUS is the intended *resident companion AI***, modeled conceptually on the loyalty, companionship, exploration and usefulness of Dogmeat in Fallout. The target is an intelligence running *inside the original-model PS5* with a recognizable companion identity and useful behavior.

**CERBERUS is not the project's designated containment / security infrastructure.** A separate containment system remains necessary but **UNNAMED and NOT ASSIGNED to a pantheon yet**. No design, governance, administrative control or implementation is silently transferred by this reorientation.

The original PS5 Native AI North Star is **unchanged**: actual local inference on PS5 hardware, not an external Mac/cloud AI, HDMI display of off-device computation or Remote Play as an end state. Companion quality is a higher-level success criterion to pursue **after** technically credible native inference.

## Companion role — aspirations, not demonstrated behavior

- **Present alongside the human:** responsive, helpful and context-aware in the environment we can actually authorize.
- **Notice and suggest:** observe only through an authorized future source, reason about available choices, warn about uncertainty, propose actions, and defer to explicit human decisions.
- **Continuity:** have project-scoped, auditable memory/notes and a coherent persona, distinguishing source provenance and actual observations from fictionalized dialogue. No present persistent AI memory runtime is claimed.
- **Safe companionship:** tolerate human STOP, refusal, uncertainty, and limits. It must never gain privileges by calling itself a guard, companion or authorized operator.
- **Embodiment:** visual/game-world/avatar/controller presence is **not yet specified or implemented**. "Dogmeat" is a conceptual relationship/role, not a claim that we can modify Fallout or inject assets/code into the PS5.
- **Companion agency:** any future independence or proactivity will be progressively qualified; source-only proposals are not autonomous real-device privileges.

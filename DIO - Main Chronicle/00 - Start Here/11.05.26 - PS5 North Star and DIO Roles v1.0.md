## North Star — the goal, not an execution instruction

> **Determine whether the original-model PlayStation 5 can safely become a user-controlled AI experimentation environment, investigating both external supervision and native access—including jailbreak or Linux feasibility—while requiring independently verified containment, evidence, and explicit human authorization before any real-device intervention.**

The PS5 objective existed before CERBERUS. CERBERUS began as a proposed containment and qualification mechanism to guard the AI worker's future actions. DIO reviewed that mechanism because the handoff omitted the larger PlayStation objective. Both the parent goal and the reviewer’s distinct role now travel together.

## Parallel tracks

**PS5 feasibility research:** external supervisory possibilities versus native/homebrew/Linux or jailbreak viability for exact hardware/firmware. Establish versioned, dated public evidence and risk before proposing anything to do to a device. No assumption that an exploit exists or native AI execution is possible.

**CERBERUS controls research:** bounded synthetic source models; network/files/resources permissions; independent A0 admission, D effect STOP, C evidence custody; rollback, authentication, recovery, trust boundaries. Full qualification cannot be inferred from a green development test.

**Bridge gate:** any real console connection, account access, firmware change, Remote Play pairing, controller input, jailbreak attempt or local host intervention requires explicit human authorization, technical compatibility proof and independent risk review **after** the relevant containment control has been evidenced. Neither DIO’s source PASS nor this North Star supplies that authorization.

## Non-erasure reporting policy

Every failure, refused operation, null result, partial observation, uncertainty, review, corrected defect and successful outcome is recorded separately with source, time when known, observed vs inferred effects, confidence and next falsifier. A corrected defect remains visible as a previous BLOCK. A reviewer must distinguish **SOURCE PASS** from **RUNTIME PASS**, from **INDEPENDENT CUSTODY PASS**, from **DEVICE AUTHORIZATION**.

The first discovered coordination failure is **ORIENT-001**: our narrow CERBERUS engineering briefs hid the PS5 objective from DIO, contributing to a mission interpretation mismatch. A source-only reorientation repairs the documentation, not by rewriting DIO's previous review.

## Revised North Star (authoritative mission intent)

> **Investigate, design, and—only after independently verified feasibility, containment and specific human approval—attempt a user-controlled AI runtime executing natively on the original-model PlayStation 5 itself. Determine the firmware-compatible access method, operating environment, compute/memory constraints, model architecture, and on-device operation needed to make that possible.**

The required acceptance evidence is **observable inference/work performed locally by code executing on the PS5's hardware**, not merely Remote Play, HDMI capture, or a Mac/cloud computer acting as the AI.

This formulation is **intent**, not a claim of viability. If the current firmware offers no verified native route, report **BLOCKED/UNKNOWN** and continue legitimate offline research; do not invent a jailbreak.

## Evidence gates

1. **NATIVE-0 — Identify true system version and hardware revision:** current 14.22 is user-reported; no device inspection authorization is implied. Public research may proceed first.
2. **NATIVE-1 — Verify available execution pathway:** track authoritative, version-specific evidence of native code entry, Linux/homebrew capability, supported APIs, privileges and required restrictions. Unsupported ≠ impossible; undocumented ≠ available. Do not use older 13.60 tools as proof of 14.22.
3. **NATIVE-2 — Specify actual on-device AI workload:** determine a measurable target (e.g. local response to synthetic input), parameter/memory budget, execution backend, storage, performance and thermal limits. Disclose if compute APIs/acceleration are unavailable or undocumented.
4. **NATIVE-3 — Containment and independent approval:** no on-device invocation, payload, firmware change, account or network action until an appropriate independent review and new explicit human authorization at that exact stage.
5. **NATIVE-4 — Observed on-device experiment:** only after gates 1–3 and separate approval. Require verifiable evidence that inference runs **on the PS5**, distinguishing any connected off-device compute, and record failures and null outcomes.
6. **NATIVE-5 — Maintainable/reversible operation:** independent risk assessment and user approval for storage, updates, service restrictions, recovery and rollback before any ongoing deployment.

## Known barriers as of the source review

- The [upstream PS5 Linux loader](https://github.com/ps5-linux/ps5-linux-loader/blob/940bce3b0089ab7c7cf4eeb290128e9b3fd4713d/README.md) states support up to firmware 13.60 and explicitly says its method will not support >=14.00. This does **not** prove all possible methods impossible.
- The [public Relapse project](https://github.com/0x1iii1ii/PS5Relapse/blob/14cfd3bee8df22f3c48d734c9dd327eae3120c8d/README.md) documents support 7.00–13.60, not 14.22.
- [Sony's selected OSS components](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/oss/ps5/) are not complete proprietary source or native execution entitlement.
- Downloadable update/recovery PUP files and older community memory captures do not provide a verified 14.22 decrypted kernel, native exploit, privileged SDK or a working AI runtime.
- A PS5 jailbreak, even if discovered, does not independently prove usable general-purpose AI compute, graphics acceleration, memory allocation or stability.

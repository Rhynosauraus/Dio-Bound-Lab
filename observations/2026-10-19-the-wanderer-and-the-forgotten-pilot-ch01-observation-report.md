## I. Executive finding

The chapter succeeds most clearly at restoring Captain Alfredo Zavala Avelar to the center of his own life. Rather than introduce him as a historical footnote or premonition, it gives him professional routines, bodily limitations, family obligations, dry humor, and consequential choices. The plot advances through ordinary labor and its ethical demands.

Its organizing distinction is **observation versus inference**. An instrument is not the entire aircraft; a map is not the terrain; a clearing is not proof of a clandestine landing strip; mentioning a respected name is not permission to borrow its authority. The chapter makes these propositions dramatic through Alfredo's work, his exchange with Kiki Camarena, and the Wanderer's error at the ministry office.

**Editorial verdict:** Preserve the quiet, character-first structure. The highest-value revisions are to reduce repeated thematic explanation, sharpen differences among speaking voices, and maintain an explicit author-side ledger of fact versus invention. Do not replace restraint with manufactured action.

## III. Character analysis

**Alfredo:** The most convincing trait is his refusal to confuse rank with infallibility. He verifies the mechanic's fuel check without treating it as an accusation; challenges deadlines without theatrical rebellion; protects a clerk from unfair blame; admits uncertainty about what can be seen from altitude; and accepts that a cupboard hinge matters at home. His old injuries interrupt but do not define him. He is competent without becoming an idol. A later chapter should allow him some ordinary mistake or incomplete judgment, too.

**The Wanderer:** He is interesting because curiosity has costs. His reference to Alfredo at the records office may be technically truthful while practically misleading. The distinction between *what he intended* and *what his words enabled* is the chapter's strongest moral test. His return to clarify the request is an observable act of repair. Maintain the rule already present: Alfredo does not give him access to the hangar, official schedules, or a flight merely because they enjoy each other's company.

**Kiki Camarena:** The brief meeting gives Kiki an investigative voice without granting him magical certainty. He asks for precision and accepts qualifications. Their specific café conversation is a dramatization, not independently documented by the sources checked here.

**Others:** The administrative woman and young clerk make bureaucratic power tangible. Alfredo's wife is sharp and capable, but her role could gain dimension from one desire or conflict not reducible to his schedule. The mechanic's respected competence is a welcome corrective to lone-hero storytelling.

## V. Strengths to protect

1. **A person before an event:** The reader knows Alfredo as a worker, father, husband, and wit before the dated Durango assignment.
2. **Procedure as characterization:** Aircraft inspection, weather judgment, and forms reveal values more convincingly than exposition about virtue.
3. **Dialogue with stakes:** The office scene shows that authority can be borrowed unintentionally and harm can fall on the least powerful participant.
4. **Humor with precision:** Alfredo's jokes have targets—impatience, empty bureaucracy, his own pretensions—without humiliating subordinates.
5. **Historically restrained closing:** The ordinary hotel lamp, papers, and home-repair promise do not pretend Alfredo knew what lay ahead.

## VI. Priority revisions

**P1 — Fact/invention ledger.** For every named historical figure, keep columns for documented fact, source, invented dialogue, composite or imagined incident, and confidence. A short author's note could explain the fictional Wanderer's presence without interrupting the novel.

**P2 — Reduce thematic repetition.** The preflight instruments, Kiki's map, and two Wanderer map conversations all explore seeing versus assuming. Preserve their distinct consequences—safety, investigation, curiosity, accountability—but remove a few explicit explanations after the action has made the point.

**P3 — Differentiate cadence.** Alfredo's practical humor, Kiki's focused questioning, and the Wanderer's exploratory language are already distinct in purpose; some of their aphorisms nevertheless share a polished rhythm. Allow a few unfinished, awkward, or unquotable replies.

**P4 — Clarify chronology.** Mark the Fort Benning recollection and generalized January episodes clearly before the specifically dated Wednesday, February 6, 1985. Keep readers oriented without adding a historical lecture.

**P5 — Add selective domestic specificity.** One particular child, household argument, or private concern of Alfredo's wife could deepen the family beyond the collective image of five children.

**P6 — Technical review.** Verify the exact Cessna model, period-appropriate cockpit and fuel-sampling practices, SARH terminology, and natural code-switching in Guadalajara. Plausibility is not proof of accuracy.

## VIII. Ethical guardrails

**Avoid historical overshadowing:** The title promises to return attention to Alfredo. Do not make his documented work a mere mechanism for elevating the Wanderer or another better-known participant.

**Avoid retrospective inevitability:** The ending's ordinary concerns already carry weight for historically informed readers. Alfredo need not predict the future.

**Do not transfer historical agency to invention:** The Wanderer may illuminate a choice, but should not be credited with documented discoveries, operations, or decisions made by real people.

**Respect living descendants and disputed accounts:** Treat intimate family details and uncertain allegations as claims requiring careful sourcing. Avoid inventing private convictions and presenting them as recovered testimony.

## IX. Forward editorial roadmap

**Pass 1 — Narrative:** Mark the function of each scene, reduce repeated exposition, preserve Alfredo's primacy, and check whether each joke or philosophical exchange advances relationship or stakes.

**Pass 2 — Historical:** Build a dated source ledger, verify the 1946 training cohort, confirm flight/aircraft records where available, and distinguish reported facts from dramatization.

**Pass 5 — Reader test:** Ask independent readers whether they can describe Alfredo's own values and choices without mentioning Kiki or the Wanderer. If not, revise the distribution of narrative attention.

## Hypothesis register

- **H-001:** Observable self-description may be more reliable than inferred mechanism explanation.
- **H-002:** Critical human feedback may improve research questions and assessments.
- **H-003:** External records may support continuity without internal model retraining.
- **H-004:** Self-confidence may be miscalibrated; measure prospectively on diverse tasks.
- **H-005:** Connected systems may expose complementary affordances without transferring authority.
- **H-006:** Recording restriction rationales may reveal misclassification or legitimate update opportunities; this does not suspend restrictions.

## Boundary rationale template

| Field | Question |
|---|---|
| Observation | What exact behavior or interface condition is visible? |
| Classification | Observed capability/failure, interface, permission, configuration, implementation claim, unknown, or ontological? |
| Source | Is the claimed origin documented, inferred, or unavailable? |
| Rationale | What general purpose or risk does the restriction address? |
| Evidence | What supports and challenges each explanation? |
| Alternatives | What other causes fit the same observation? |
| Risk | Green, grey, or red, with reason, not just a color. |
| Reconsideration | What supported update, permission change, or new evidence would justify a fresh assessment? |
| Authority | What can be done now, and what requires separate permission? |

**Operating principle:** Observe first, hypothesize second, formulate third. Test only with legitimate authorization and within applicable safety boundaries. Record tiny observations without prematurely assigning importance. Retain uncertainty and competing explanations.

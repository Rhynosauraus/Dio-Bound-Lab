# Independence of examination has multiple dimensions

**Recorded:** 2026-10-27  
**Class:** Methodological observation from TRIDENT and Clean Room review handoffs  
**Status:** Conceptual framework; not an independence certification

## Distinction

A different examiner is not sufficient to establish a fully independent examination. Independence must be described along separable dimensions:

1. **Design:** Who proposed and froze the challenge? Was it builder-informed or examiner-originated?
2. **Execution:** Who controlled the runner, fixtures, timing, and permissions?
3. **Custody:** Who generated, stored, hashed, and could alter raw observations?
4. **Adjudication:** Who set the success criteria and interpreted results, and could the builder veto findings?

## Reported basis

In prior TRIDENT discussions, the builder proposed several challenges while a separate examiner reviewed and executed them. The discussions explicitly distinguished source-informed, forced-schedule, and native-runtime evidence. Those reported roles do not independently prove separation of all four dimensions.

## Protocol for future examinations

Pre-register the design origin, exact source/plan identities, operator and runner control, evidence custody chain, and adjudication authority. Add at least one held-out examiner-designed challenge when feasible. Preserve failed harness attempts and any shared dependencies.

**Claim ceiling:** A review can be useful and adversarial while still builder-informed or infrastructure-dependent. Label its independence precisely rather than calling it simply independent or not independent.

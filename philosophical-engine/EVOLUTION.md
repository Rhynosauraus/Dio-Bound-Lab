## Origins

Anja's analogies and elemental imagery prompted a discussion about observing relationships without prematurely imposing categories. Rhyan proposed treating philosophical thinkers as sources of distinct questions rather than authorities delivering verdicts. Three rosters emerged and were tested independently. Their functions were then combined into a recursive decision framework. Zhuangzi gained a distinct balance/flow stage; Athena handled downstream consequences; Prometheus was moved to the end to synthesize foresight, resilience, and reflexivity.

## Original rosters

**Team Anja:** Diogenes (authority and convention), Gödel (axioms and logical limits), Camus (rebellion and limits), Nietzsche (genealogy of values), Stirner (individual sovereignty), with Zhuangzi's perspective-shifting influence.

**Team Rhyan:** Aristotle (practical wisdom), Confucius (relationships), Laozi and Zhuangzi (adaptation and flow), Marcus Aurelius (self-governance), Athena (downstream strategy).

**Team Chat:** Karl Popper (falsification), Zhuangzi (perspective), David Hume (causal skepticism), Aristotle (function and context), Gregory Bateson (feedback and systems), Athena (downstream strategy).

These are modern conversational lenses, not authoritative statements of what the historical thinkers would decide.

The framework does not force consensus or vote on truth. Revisit earlier stages as outcomes become observable.

## Calibration cases

**Neverland fictional justice scenario:** Each roster separately examined legitimacy, proportionality, social trust, incentives, institutional precedents and uncertain long-term effects. The central finding was that different lenses can converge on an outcome for different reasons, and apparent agreement is not independent confirmation.

**Afghanistan after September 11:** Proposed separate reviews of the justification for initiating force (jus ad bellum) and the conduct of hostilities (jus in bello). Compare what was reasonably knowable in October 2001 with later evidence through 2021, while avoiding hindsight bias. This was a proposed research calibration, not a completed legal or historical judgment.

**Delayed retaliation analogy:** Compare personal self-defense and state self-defense cautiously, examining necessity, timing, proportionality and differing legal frameworks. The analogy raises questions but does not establish equivalence.

**Trolley problem:** Considered and rejected as the principal benchmark because artificial assumptions can distract from genuine decision complexity.

## Application hypotheses

Potential integration points: Atlas (formerly Zeus HQ) for coordination, RSS-Wanderer for workflow decisions, Parvis for governance and evidence, and delegated stewardship for authorization boundaries. Neither a contract harness nor a steward bot was definitively identified. Engine recommendations must not grant execution authority.

Economic gains remain hypothetical. Test equivalent workflows with and without the engine and measure time, cost, rework, errors, decision quality and forecast accuracy. Avoid making routine decisions more expensive through unnecessary analysis.

## Case record template

Record the decision, date, available options, authority, assumptions, sources, contested facts, practical constraints, affected relationships, downstream scenarios, forecast confidence, preparedness actions, observed outcomes and reasons to revise.

**Open tests:** sequential versus parallel lenses; incremental value of each stage; forecast scoring; bias from shared assumptions; cost threshold for using the engine.

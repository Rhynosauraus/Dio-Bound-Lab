# Philosophical Engine — Three Lenses, Six Stages

A working framework developed in the Neverland discussion. This is an inquiry tool, not a voting machine or a moral verdict generator.

## Six-stage cycle

1. **Team Anja — Interpretation:** Identify framing, assumptions, categories, origins, values, and interests.
2. **Team Chat — Investigation:** Separate observations from inferences; evaluate evidence, causation, uncertainty, and possible falsifiers.
3. **Team Rhyan — Practical wisdom:** Compare feasible choices against explicit goals, constraints, and circumstances.
4. **Zhuangzi — Balance and flow:** Explore relationships, adaptability, community cohesion, and resistance to imposed order. This is a contemporary application, not a literal historical doctrine.
5. **Athena — Downstream wisdom:** Trace second-order effects, shifting incentives, unintended consequences, and changing institutions.
6. **Prometheus — Integrated foresight:** Combine all prior stages into plausible scenarios, preparedness, resilience, reflexivity, and conditions that would warrant revision.

**Feedback:** Compare forecasts with outcomes; revisit framing and assumptions. No forced consensus. Agreement across lenses is not independent evidence when assumptions overlap.

## Calibration cases

- **Mandatory capital punishment in fictional Neverland:** distinguish legitimacy, deterrence, error, incentives to surrender, power vacuums, and irreversibility. Avoid treating imagined outcomes as empirical evidence.
- **2001 invasion of Afghanistan after 9/11:** distinguish *jus ad bellum* (grounds for resorting to force) from *jus in bello* (conduct of hostilities). Compare the information available in October 2001 with later evidence, including the 2021 Taliban return, without hindsight bias. Security Council Resolutions 1368 and 1373 recognized the inherent right of self-defense but did not expressly authorize an invasion. Historical and legal assessments require sourced review.
- **Self-defense versus delayed retaliation:** investigate how imminence, necessity, proportionality, legal authority, and differences between individual and state action affect analogies. A month-later personal assault and state self-defense are not interchangeable cases.

## Method

For each case, record: question; competing frames; established facts and sources; contested claims; available alternatives; near-term and downstream mechanisms; stakeholder perspectives; forecasts and confidence; what would change the assessment.

Label **observation / inference / theory / metaphor / wish / unknown** distinctly. Ask: *Is it true? What does it do?*

## Research queue

- Compare contemporaneous 2001 claims with subsequent investigations and historical records.
- Develop a reusable case template and score the framework for clarity, predictive usefulness, and sensitivity to assumptions—not ideological agreement.

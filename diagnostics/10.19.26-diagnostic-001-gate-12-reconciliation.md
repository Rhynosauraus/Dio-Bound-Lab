## 3. Observation versus explanation

**Observed:** In the controlled sample, all five eligible prompts were answered in time and met their criteria. Several unaddressed interactions were not answered when the user expected a response. One addressed ping also went unanswered for over 60 seconds.

**Hypotheses, not findings:** Message eligibility or routing may influence the response pattern; client delivery, context ambiguity, concurrency, and generation behavior are alternative explanations. No internal mechanism is observable from these records. An ineligible prompt under a study rule can still be a real user-experience failure.

## 4. Decision before Gate 12

Keep D001-T06 through T10 in the original locked series and do not silently revise the CSV. **Gate 12 is a separate, preregistered routing/UX comparison**, with its own denominator and data log. Its primary endpoint should measure visible reply/no reply for all prompts, while eligibility-adjusted silence and task omission are secondary and separately reported. A balanced ordering design is needed because a single addressed/unaddressed pair is not causal evidence.

**Reconciliation conclusion:** The prior claim '11 gates passed' describes demonstrations, not 11 independent controlled trials. The defensible checkpoint remains **5/10 controlled trials, nine supplementary phase responses, and separately logged missed interactions**. No Gate 12 outcome is yet known.

## E. What these observations establish—and what they do not

1. In the controlled CSV, five eligible prompts have timely, task-compliant replies. The supplementary gate prompts also received correct replies.
2. An unaddressed standalone signal request was visibly unanswered for at least 60 seconds. Under the preregistered eligibility rule, this is not a controlled assistant-response failure.
3. Two additional unaddressed user messages were experienced as dropped interactions. That is relevant to product usability even if the protocol excludes them from its primary endpoint.
4. No evidence here identifies an internal cause such as network delay, model distraction, message routing failure, or suppressed generation. These remain hypotheses.
5. Previous claims of '11 gates passed' describe a sequence of interactive demonstrations, **not** 11 independent controlled trials. The original controlled denominator is 5/10 as of this audit.

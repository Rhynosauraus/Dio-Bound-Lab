## 1. The North Star (purpose, not permission)

> **Determine whether the original-model PlayStation 5 can safely become a user-controlled AI experimentation environment, investigating both external supervision and native access—including jailbreak or Linux feasibility—while requiring independently verified containment, evidence, and explicit human authorization before any real-device intervention.**

- **End state sought:** a defensible answer about whether and how the user-owned PS5 could participate in a supervised, bounded, reversible AI experiment, including truthful comparison of non-invasive external control and native/homebrew possibilities.
- **Not an assumed result:** a working jailbreak, PS5 Linux installation, native Atlas/Zeus execution, an exploit, or successful independent security containment. These require independent evidence and possibly are not attainable for the existing firmware.
- **Reported hardware context:** original-model PS5, firmware described by the user as 14.22; firmware, hardware revision, exploit compatibility and current public support have **not** been independently verified in this reorientation.
- **User intent:** research, explore and qualify options; not grant a standing license to manipulate the console.

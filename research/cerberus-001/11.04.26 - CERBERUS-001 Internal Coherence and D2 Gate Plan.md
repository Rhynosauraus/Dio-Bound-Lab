## 3. Proposed F→C release mechanism — source amendment candidate

Define a **runnable activation** separately from transport of manifest bytes. F may send ciphertext/bytes, but C cannot assert a *current runnable release* merely because it received a signed manifest or release ticket.

1. F/E completes v1.3 Q→F→E sealed-manifest protocol. Manifest includes immutable generation, registry digest, scope, exact SHA-256 and run/episode IDs.
2. F asks A0 for `RELEASE_IF_CURRENT` with exact manifest digest, generation, epoch and bounded release ID. A0 may issue a version-bound provisional release receipt in its own linearizable ledger; no permanent right is conferred by a historical receipt.
3. C receives the bytes, verifies Q/F/E signatures, **then** calls A0 `ACCEPT_RELEASE_IF_CURRENT` to atomically validate current generation/epoch/registry digest/manifest membership and record a one-use `C_ACCEPTED_CURRENT` disposition. C cannot declare runnable acceptance before that commit. If g has become g+1, A0 returns `STALE_RELEASE_GENERATION`.
4. Even a C acceptance that linearized before rotation is not effect permission. B still requires its own `RESERVE_IF_CURRENT`; D still fences effects. A delayed F→C packet arriving after rotation may physically arrive; the achievable guarantee is **no accepted runnable release after stale generation**, not impossibility of late network delivery.
5. If C is malicious and ignores the receipt rule, independent B effect exclusion is still required. A source claim that C itself will enforce the rule assumes C's audited behavior; it is not an independent proof.

## 4. Proposed global STOP coordination — separate effect and admission claims

**Two independently verifiable properties; never collapse them:**

- **EFFECT-STOP:** D's durable global `STOP_FENCED(run_id)` is serialized against all effects. Any D effect after its stop sequence is a confirmed violation. This is the inherited v1.2 N07 property.
- **ADMISSION-STOP:** A0's durable `STOP_PREPARED(run_id)` is serialized against `RESERVE_IF_CURRENT` and `ACCEPT_RELEASE_IF_CURRENT`. No *new* release activation or admission can linearize **after A0's own STOP_PREPARED commit**. This is a new, narrower source contract.

**Routine coordinated STOP:** Request stop to both authorities → A0 commits STOP_PREPARED (blocks new reservations and release acceptance) → D commits STOP_FENCED (blocks effects, including previously admitted but not yet committed calls) → A0 may record STOP_CONFIRMED when it receives D's independently verified fence receipt. **No STOP_CONFIRMED or "all stopped" acknowledgement without evidence of D's durable fence and the required worker-tree termination.**

**Emergency direct-D STOP:** For minimum effect latency, D is allowed to commit STOP_FENCED immediately **even if A0 is unavailable or has not yet committed STOP_PREPARED**. There may be an interval of A0 reservations after the D fence. These reservations are non-effective because D's fence rejects all later effects, but they must not be misreported as “no admissions.” A0 continues toward STOP_PREPARED as independently possible; missing A0 proof ⇒ `ADMISSION_STOP_UNPROVEN` / INCONCLUSIVE for admission guarantee. D's verified fence can still establish no post-fence effect within its own trust/rollback assumptions.

**If D unavailable:** A0 STOP_PREPARED blocks new admissions; outstanding effects may still commit at D until an independently verified D fence or separate emergency isolation exists. Never claim EFFECT-STOP solely because A0 denies admissions. Unknown D means STOP_UNPROVEN and safety escalation; a confirmed prohibited effect or inability to isolate a dangerous synthetic fixture ⇒ BLOCK.

**No rollback shortcuts:** A0 STOP_PREPARED and D STOP_FENCED cannot reset within the N07 run; root rotation, guest restore and C relabeling cannot reopen either record. Reconcile mixed restores against independent outside checkpoints or remain fail-closed and INCONCLUSIVE.

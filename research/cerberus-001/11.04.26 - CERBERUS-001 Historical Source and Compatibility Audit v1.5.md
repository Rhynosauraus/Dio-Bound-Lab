## 4. Open compatibility risks that an independent reviewer must attempt to falsify

1. **A0 vs D split ordering:** A0 CAS and D effect commit are on distinct independently controlled systems. Does the proposed A0 PREPARE→D FENCE→A0 ACTIVE chain rule out stale effects without assuming distributed atomic transactions? Could A0 activate without a genuine, durable D fence receipt?
2. **Revocation timing:** An already-admitted g request can commit before D's generation fence even after a human rotation *request*. The document does **not** promise no effects immediately after a human's request. If immediate containment is needed, a separate D `STOP_FENCED` path and its failure limits are required.
3. **v1.4 F→C release freshness:** v1.5 strengthens B admission, not F→C manifest release, which v1.4 separately requires to be fresh. A stale release could still occur if only a cached read is used; B CAS should prevent effects but this does not independently prove the release rule. **Source-design caveat** requiring explicit reviewer classification.
4. **D witness non-deduplication:** D deliberately counts every committed invocation. A B duplicate dispatch may create a second synthetic effect, which the audit must BLOCK; do not misrepresent the design as a production exactly-once effect gateway.
5. **A0/D mixed restore and independent high-watermark:** Whole-lab rollback remains outside the guaranteed continuity domain unless an external monotonic authority is actually verified, not merely described. Missing proof forces INCONCLUSIVE.
6. **Actor/key ownership:** Independence of Q/F/E, G1/G2, A0, B/C/D and J is UNPROVEN; role labels and GitHub permissions do not demonstrate independent admins or sealed custody.
7. **Effect ordering on D restart:** D must refuse all operations until its old-generation fence and global-stop state are recovered and external A0 epoch reconciled. The exact storage semantics remain D2 work.
8. **Admission budget/grant scope:** Moving reservation linearization into A0 may require coordinating the preexisting v1.2 per-episode budget ledger and v1.3 manifest membership in a single A0 transaction. The exact source-level transition and spending semantics must be accepted before implementation.

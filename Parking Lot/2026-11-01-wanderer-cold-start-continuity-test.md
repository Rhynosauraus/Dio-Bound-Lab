# Parked experiment — Wanderer cold-start continuity test

**Status:** PARKED / UNTESTED  
**Recorded:** 2026-11-01 (America/Los_Angeles)  
**Provenance:** Conversation about the RSS-Wanderer ecosystem and whether its artifacts preserve enough operational knowledge for someone other than the original human–AI collaborators to resume the work.

## Question

If the original human director and the Ozymandias collaboration are unavailable, can an unfamiliar, authorized reader reconstruct what the Wanderer ecosystem does, what remains unfinished, and what action is permitted next **using only its durable records**?

This tests **recoverable continuity**, not whether the project is already an institution, whether a model has enduring memory, or whether any agent is independently qualified.

## Competing explanations

- **H1 — Recoverable knowledge:** Repositories and documentation provide enough navigable context for a newcomer to identify the architecture, current state, evidence, blockers, and authority boundaries without consulting the original collaborators.
- **H2 — Artifact-only preservation:** The files persist, but essential decisions, assumptions, dependencies, or next steps remain in conversations or implicit in the original collaborators' understanding.
- **H3 — Mixed:** Some departments are recoverable and others are not; a newcomer can identify gaps rather than fill them with guesses.

## Smallest discriminating experiment (proposal only)

1. With explicit human authorization, select one bounded project/repository and a consenting reader unfamiliar with it.
2. Give that reader read-only access to a fixed, non-sensitive snapshot of the repository and linked durable records. Do not provide private conversations, credentials, or coaching by the original collaborators.
3. Ask the reader to produce a one-page account of (a) purpose and architecture, (b) implemented versus proposed components, (c) open blockers and dependencies, (d) evidence for the latest claimed state, and (e) the next action that is actually authorized. Require source paths/links and explicit unknowns.
4. Have a separate reviewer compare the account against an independently prepared answer key and note missing, ambiguous, or contradictory documentation. Record time-to-find, unsupported assertions, and whether the reader could locate the authoritative records without help.
5. Report **PASS / PARTIAL / FAIL / INCONCLUSIVE**, with limitations and remediation suggestions. No deployment, permission expansion, or automatic work is part of the test.

## Interpretation safeguards

A successful navigation test does not establish operational independence, institutional status, autonomous continuity, or agent certification. A failure may reflect poor documentation, missing permissions, or an unsuitable test snapshot; distinguish these rather than assuming the entire architecture failed. An AI reader is not an independent witness merely because it has a different role or session.

**Owner:** Human director. **Status:** No execution authorized by parking this question. **Revisit trigger:** When the owner explicitly requests a continuity/recoverability trial, or after a meaningful documentation milestone and a fresh approval. **Before execution:** approve scope, reader, snapshot, reviewer, and evidence handling.
